
# Sworna Akter

### Climate & Environment Researcher | GIS & Remote Sensing | Geospatial Analysis

Welcome to my academic research portfolio!

I am a Climate and Environment Researcher with an academic background in Geography and Environment from Jahangirnagar University, Bangladesh. My research focuses on climate extremes, heatwave characteristics and vulnerability, drought assessment, environmental monitoring, and spatial microclimate variations.

I have research experience in analyzing climatic and satellite-derived datasets, applying statistical and geospatial techniques, conducting field-based investigations, and interpreting environmental and socio-climatic patterns. I am particularly interested in integrating climate science, GIS, remote sensing, and quantitative approaches to understand environmental challenges and support evidence-based adaptation strategies.

## 📊 Research Experience

**Research Assistant | Department of Geography and Environment | Jahangirnagar University, Bangladesh | 2024–2026**

### 1. Analyzing the Characteristics of Extreme Heatwaves and Their Consequences on Agricultural Sustainability at Madhupur Tract Region of Bangladesh

*Funded by the Ministry of Science and Technology, Bangladesh*

- Analyzed climatic and satellite-derived datasets, including land surface temperature (LST), heat index, and long-term trends using statistical and spatial methods.
- Developed heatwave maps and visualizations to investigate potential agricultural impacts and farmer vulnerability, supporting adaptation-related recommendations.
- Contributed to major sections of the research report by integrating scientific analyses with field-based findings.

### 2. Unveiling the Limitations of Traditional Drought Characterisation Methods in Small-Scale Agriculture of Bangladesh

*Funded by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Bangladesh*

- Assessed drought patterns through the evaluation of long-term meteorological datasets.
- Applied principal component analysis (PCA), trend analysis, and heat-index estimation to examine the limitations of conventional drought assessment approaches.
- Interpreted survey and focus group discussion (FGD) findings, connecting scientific drought indicators with farmers' perceptions and experiences.

### 3. Exploring Micro-Climate Variations across Different Spatial Units at Jahangirnagar University

*Funded by Jahangirnagar University, Bangladesh*

- Conducted field-based microclimatic surveys across different land-use and land-cover categories.
- Implemented microclimatic zoning analysis using satellite-derived land surface temperature (LST), land use/land cover (LULC), and normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI).
- Compared field observations with web-based climatic data to investigate spatial variability and environmental patterns across different land-cover types.

 [Research Repository](https://github.com/sworna-akter/Semi-urban-Microclimate-at-JU)
